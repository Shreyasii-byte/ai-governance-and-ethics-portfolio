# AI Governance & Ethics Portfolio ⚖️

A practical portfolio exploring AI governance, responsible AI, AI ethics, AI safety, risk management, and public policy.

## 🎯 Objective

To develop practical knowledge of how organisations and governments can assess, govern, and responsibly deploy AI systems.

This portfolio follows a structured 30-day AI Governance & Ethics learning roadmap, with a focus on research, policy analysis, risk assessment, and evidence-based recommendations.

## 🧩 Core Areas

- AI Ethics and Responsible AI
- AI Risk Assessment and Mitigation
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- OECD AI Principles and UNESCO AI Ethics
- EU AI Act and AI Regulation
- AI Impact Assessments
- AI Red Teaming and Evaluation
- Corporate Responsible AI Governance
- AI Safety and Policy Research

## 🔍 Central Case Study

**AI-Powered Recruitment System**

A consistent case study used to examine:

- Algorithmic bias and discrimination
- Candidate privacy and data protection
- Transparency and explainability
- Hallucinations and inaccurate information
- Human oversight and accountability
- AI safety and responsible deployment
